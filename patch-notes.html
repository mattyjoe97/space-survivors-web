SPACE SURVIVORS PATCH NOTES

Current version 3.82 · Play in your browser

3.82 Walks you can see

2026-09-26

  • Walking enemies now waddle, bob and lurch with every step, so their walk shows at normal zoom (the leg movement alone was too small to notice).

3.81 The Forge Titan speaks for itself

2026-09-26

  • The Forge Titan announces its own phases (The furnace roars, Molten core exposed, MELTDOWN) instead of the Eclipse Colossus's lines.

3.80 Enemies walk

2026-09-26

  • The Void Stalker, Rift Skitter, Obsidian Warden and Void Ravager now really walk: their legs step in time with how fast they move, instead of sliding along the ground.
  • The Void Ravager and the Commander stomp toward you.

3.79 Smoother hordes

2026-09-26

  • Big hordes run faster: enemies work out how to spread apart in one shared pass, and calm enemies update their looks a little less often. On the test laptop the biggest planet 1 horde went from about 35 to 39 frames a second.

3.78 Elites stand up

2026-09-26

  • The Void Ravager, the Commander and the Core Warden no longer look like they are lying on their sides or backs: they stay upright and sway as they stomp toward you.

3.77 Forge Titan

2026-09-26

  • The Forge Titan (the Foundry's final boss) is beatable with a strong build: it has less health against high-level ships and its body hits less hard when it touches you. It is still the hardest fight in the game.

3.76 Aegis starts stronger

2026-09-26

  • The Photon Scattergun (Aegis's signature weapon) hits harder and fires faster at low levels, so Aegis no longer crawls through its first minutes. At max level it is only a little stronger than before.

3.75 Music level

2026-09-26

  • The new music plays at the same volume as the old soundtrack, so it no longer drowns out the sound effects.
  • Damage numbers are drawn more efficiently (they look the same).

3.74 Pathing and new upgrade icons

2026-09-26

  • Every augment and upgrade card has its own icon now (they used to share a gear or an arrow): a flame for Thermal Reactor, a snowflake for Cryogenic Manifold, a charged cell for Capacitor Bank, an hourglass for Temporal Drag, and so on. The HUD shows them too.
  • Enemies now steer round rocks and ruins in their way instead of piling up behind them, and they no longer get stuck in pockets between rocks.
  • A crowd splits round an obstacle on the side you are on, so it still reaches you as one wave.

3.73 New music

2026-09-26

  • A new soundtrack, composed and synthesized in code for this game: a calm piece for the main menu, a dark synthwave piece for the Obsidian Expanse, an industrial piece for the Foundry, and a boss theme.
  • The planet music has a calm layer and a combat layer: the drums, bass and lead come in once the fight starts.
  • Each piece is a minute or so long instead of the old 16-second loop.

3.72 Fairer boss, Hard and Brutal, new codex

2026-09-26

  • The Eclipse Colossus fight is fairer: its close-range slam waits a little longer, reaches less far and hits less hard, and touching the boss now throws you clear instead of hitting again and again. Its health grows a little less with your level.
  • Hard and Brutal difficulties, per ship: beat the Eclipse Colossus with a ship to open Hard for it, then beat it on Hard to open Brutal. Pick it with the DIFFICULTY button in the hangar. Hard: enemies have 50% more health and hit 30% harder, score x1.5. Brutal: double health, 60% harder hits, more enemies, score x2.
  • The hangar shows which difficulties each ship has beaten the Colossus on.
  • The codex is rebuilt: every enemy of both planets with its real art and what it does (dark until you meet it), the weapons, and all eight evolutions with what each one needs.
  • The Foundry's small spinning drones and exploding cargo pods are now called Saw Drone and Cargo Brood in run reports.

3.71 Achievements unlock things

2026-09-26

  • No more permanent stat upgrades: the Armory, contracts and missions are gone. Instead, 15 achievements each unlock something: weapons, ships, relics, perks and modes (Progression > Achievements shows every goal, your progress and what it unlocks).
  • A new player starts with Nova, the Plasma Lance, Hunter Missiles, Graviton Mines and Cryo Field, plus three relics. Locked weapons and relics never show up in level-ups or at the merchant.
  • Ships unlock by playing: beat the Eclipse Colossus for the Voidrunner, evolve 3 different weapons for the Tempest, reach the Foundry with 2 ships for the Aegis.
  • New perks to earn: +1 reroll every run, Second Chance (once a run, a lethal hit leaves you at 30% hull) and Warden Radar (an arrow to the Core Warden).
  • Endless mode unlocks by beating the Eclipse Colossus; beating the Forge Titan unlocks Planet Select (start a run at the Foundry from the hangar).
  • The game-over and victory screens announce anything a run unlocks. Existing saves get credit for what they already did.
  • YOUR BUILD now shows what each weapon still needs to evolve (for example: needs Phase Matrix T3), and run reports say how the boss fight went.
  • Fixed: the merchant's prices and its once-a-run reroll token carried over into your next run.

3.70.1 Send feedback

2026-09-25

  • SEND FEEDBACK in the main menu and the pause screen opens a short playtest survey. Your game version, device, ship and last run report are filled in for you, and nothing is sent until you press Submit. No name or email needed.

3.70 Dash rework and playtest fixes

2026-09-25

  • The dash goes about twice as far and faster, far enough to break out of a crowd. Its path lights up, the landing rings out, and Nova's Overcharge now also blasts the landing spot clear.
  • Evolving a weapon blasts nearby enemies back and makes you invulnerable for a moment while the new weapon spins up.
  • The Dimensional Reaper keeps the tier 5 Void Blades ring under its glaives, so evolving the blades never leaves you weaker up close.
  • Abyss Spitters no longer kite perfectly: they back off at half speed, stay a bit closer, and hold still for a moment after each spit. The Foundry's ranged enemies back off more slowly too.
  • Field Emitters, Ablative Plating, Nanite Repair and Thruster Capacitors now say what they do on their cards.
  • Long augment texts (like Phase Matrix) no longer run into the level number on their cards.
  • Run reports and YOUR BUILD now list modifiers and relics, like Energy Siphon.

3.69 New HUD and your build at a glance

2026-09-25

  • A cleaner HUD, laid out like the genre's best: the XP bar runs across the top of the screen, your level and kills sit top-right, and your weapons and augments sit top-left with tier bars under each.
  • Your hull bar and dash charges now sit right under your ship, where your eyes already are. The hull bar glows green while it repairs.
  • Weapons are shown with their own turret art everywhere: the HUD, the level-up cards and the codex.
  • YOUR BUILD: the level-up and pause screens show your weapons, augments, tunings and ship stats on the left, so you can compare a card with what you already have. The K stats window is gone.
  • The pause screen shows your score, kills, planet and sector, and the control keys.
  • The main menu is tidier: Patch Notes, Credits and Copy Run Reports share a smaller block above Quit.

3.68 Surviving the early game

2026-09-25

  • Enemies hit harder more slowly: their damage now reaches full strength around 13 minutes instead of 6.
  • Elites and the Commander mini-boss hit in line with the time of the run: the first elite (2:00) and the Commander (3:00) no longer take a third of Nova's hull in one touch.
  • Hull repair: every ship slowly repairs itself after 3 seconds without taking damage. The hull bar glows green while it repairs. Nanite Repair still makes it faster.
  • Fixed: near a Null Leech (or a Siphon Drone on the Foundry) the ship flashed and beeped constantly and was briefly immune to other hits. Their drains now wear the hull down steadily.
  • Run reports now show the game version, what dealt the damage you took (by enemy) and how much hull you repaired.
  • Patch notes: this screen, also on the web next to the browser game.

3.67.3 Version number

2026-09-25

  • The main menu shows the game version in the bottom-right corner.
  • The browser version always loads the newest update after a refresh.

3.67.2 Copy reports

2026-09-25

  • COPY REPORT and COPY RUN REPORTS copy straight to the clipboard, with no download. If the browser blocks it, a box opens with the text selected: press Ctrl+C.

3.67.1 Share your runs

2026-09-25

  • The game-over and victory screens have COPY REPORT, and the main menu COPY RUN REPORTS (your last 5 runs), ready to paste into a chat.

3.67 Void Blades rework, easier early game, new practice tool

2026-09-25

  • Void Blades only orbit now: a wide, calm ring round the ship. Each tier adds a blade, size and damage (2 to 6 blades).
  • Dimensional Reaper: two big glaives orbit the ship and are thrown on a steady beat, straight out and back through a target. Dashing hurls both along the dash.
  • The early game is easier: enemies speed up and hit harder over 6 minutes instead of 3, no Elite Hunt or Void Storm before 3:00, and Fracture Brood children are no stronger than their parent.
  • Nova has more hull (90), and its dash now blasts nearby enemies away.
  • DEV LAB (practice tool) redone: every enemy with its art, where and how many to spawn, real waves, a target dummy that shows DPS.

3.66 Hellfire Lance

2026-09-25

  • Hellfire Lance is a heavy beam that sweeps across the pack and leaves burning ground. On an elite or the boss it holds and melts the target.

3.65 Plasma Lance bolts

2026-09-25

  • The Plasma Lance fires fast piercing plasma bolts instead of a beam: more shots and pierce as it levels up.
  • Nova's Overcharge dash supercharges the next 10 Lance shots.

3.64.1 Joystick

2026-09-25

  • The drag joystick is on the left mouse button, and its ring stays where you pressed.

3.64 Railgun looks

2026-09-25

  • The Railgun fires a thin streak that fades in a blink and is never drawn over the ship; the turret glows while it charges and kicks back when it fires.

3.63 Weapons aim themselves

2026-09-25

  • Every weapon aims itself, with four targeting modes: SMART, NEAREST, STRONGEST and FORWARD. Middle mouse or Tab switches between them in a run.
  • Turrets turn toward their targets, and a drag joystick steers the ship with the mouse or touch.

3.62 Performance

3.60 New enemy art

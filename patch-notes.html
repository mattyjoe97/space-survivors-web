ORBIT OVERRUN PATCH NOTES

Current version 4.30 · Play in your browser

4.30 A fairer early game, lighter late crowds

2026-10-02

  • Ordinary enemies late in a run now have much less health, so the crowd is no longer a wall of sponges. Armor is now the exception: fewer enemies roll it, and late armor lets more damage through.
  • Surviving the first minutes is easier: a hit gives you 0.65 seconds of protection from further hits (it was 0.5; drains still get through), Void Stalkers on the first planet hit a little softer, defensive upgrades (hull, armor, repair, life steal) show up about twice as often before level 10, your hull starts repairing 2 seconds after a hit (it was 3), and the Energy Siphon can heal between the hits of a chain.
  • Elites and Commanders now hold out for a base of 14 and 16 seconds against a very strong build (it was 18 and 20; stun, stagger and Relay bonuses can still shorten it), and they flash when your shots are being held back so you can see why. The Commander's Rally Banner comes every 20 seconds instead of 14.
  • Auto-aim (SMART) now gives an elite that cannot take more damage yet a lower priority and works on the crowd while it recovers; an enemy you focus still comes first.
  • Fixed: the Colossus, Titan, Hammer Elite, Core Wardens and the Heavy Crawler ram no longer throw you across the screen when their hit did not actually land on you (the Void Ravager gore toss is unchanged).
  • Fixed: Second Chance and Emergency Shield stay used when you save and continue a run (they came back before). Their protection and the protection after evolving also last their full time against drains, and a continued run keeps the protection you had left.
  • Fixed: the Titan's flak cannon and the Artillerist's darts no longer use up their damage allowance on hits that did not land.
  • Fixed: the practice tool Strength preview shows the crowd health a real run spawns.
  • Smoother frames when many enemies die at once: on our test PC the slowest frames in a 300-kill burst are about three times better, and the Titan fight and Tempest's dash also hold their slowest frames better.
  • Known issue: the Eclipse Colossus fight can run a little slower than in 4.29 for some builds, mostly with Absolute Zero Field (its freezing and shattering of many enemies at once). We are looking at it.

4.29 Sharper kills

2026-10-01

  • Enemies now die in a quick, clear gold pop, and an enemy your direct shots hit but do not kill flinches back from the hit.
  • New in Settings (main menu and pause menu): Kill Effects, NEW or CLASSIC. NEW is the default; CLASSIC brings back the old death effects without the flinch. Sounds and screen shake are the same as before in both.
  • Damage Numbers in Settings is now ON or OFF. OFF hides the numbers over enemies only: your repair and hull-loss numbers always show (the old switch hid those too).
  • Resonance Cascade now hits a lone target twice: when there is only one enemy in reach, a second resonant strike follows a moment later.
  • Fixed: on the Foundry, a Siphon Drone could repair an elite, a Commander or a Core Warden faster than even a strong build could hurt it, so one standing near a drone was almost impossible to kill. Drones now repair ordinary enemies only.

4.28 Elites and bosses stand and fight

2026-10-01

  • Elites, Commanders, Core Wardens and bosses now last long enough to show their moves. A very strong build can no longer melt them in a second or two: each has a minimum fight time (elites 18 seconds, Commanders 20, Core Wardens 30, the Eclipse Colossus 45, the Forge Titan 70). Weaker builds are not held back: their damage lands in full.
  • Stunning, staggering or exposing them, and the Targeting Relay's mark, still make them take extra damage.
  • Late elites, Commanders and Core Wardens now grow tougher along with the crowd around them, instead of falling behind it after the first few minutes.
  • The Shadow Lunge and other big hits no longer fling you across the screen: knockback from bosses and elites is smaller and capped, a boss only throws you back when its touch actually hurts you, and a dash through an attack never throws you.
  • With a strong build the Eclipse Colossus fight now lasts longer, so expect a tougher fight.
  • Fixed: a rare bug could leave the game stuck in slow motion after two big kills close together.

4.27 Enemies keep up, smoother frames, new blade icons

2026-09-30

  • Fixed: if you flew far ahead in one direction, enemies could stop arriving and the run went quiet. Enemies left far behind now come back in from the edges of the screen, so the fight keeps up with you.
  • Smoother frames in big fights: enemy animations, hit flashes, burning effects and damage numbers cost less every frame.
  • Void Blades and the Dimensional Reaper have new icons that match their blades in battle, and weapon and ship pictures on cards and menus look smoother at small sizes. In the hangar, the Void Blades badge slowly turns.
  • New attack poses for the Foundry's elites: the Hammer Elite raises and drops its hammer and drives its bulldozer blade, the Foundry Commander rallies and braces behind its shield, and the Slag Bombard loads its barrage and overcharges its shell.

4.26 The Wardens become mini-bosses

2026-09-30

  • The first planet's Core Warden is now the CROWN BREAKER: it drops in and fights with three big physical attacks, one at a time. EXECUTION PUNCH: it draws its fist back, steps in and punches (dash sideways). CROWN CAST: it tears off its crown, lifts it overhead and throws it down a marked line (step or dash off the line). GUARDIAN'S FALL: it rises, picks your spot and smashes down on it (dash away once it commits).
  • The Foundry's Core Warden is now the SIEGE COFFIN, a siege tank that uses its own armour as ammunition. TREAD KICK: it rocks onto one tread and kicks the other out sideways (dash away from the raised tread). HATCH HAMMER: it flings its hatch down a marked line (step or dash off the line). SIEGE DROP: it rocket-hops onto your spot and belly-flops (dash away once it leaves the ground).
  • Every Warden attack is shown before it lands and can be dodged; a dash is always an answer. Other enemies stop arriving during a Warden's big attack. At half health each Warden cracks open and changes its look.
  • The Warden's health bar moves up out of the way while it holds its crown overhead.
  • The Forge Titan's body now animates as it hovers, breathes fire, vents and melts down.

4.25 Clearer tunings, Tempest's new dash, a new look

2026-09-30

  • Tuning cards now tell you what they will do for your current build, and warn you when one would not help it. Level-up cards repeat less and are easier to read.
  • The merchant is tidier: prices in their own column and easier-to-read descriptions.
  • Tempest's dash is new: dash past enemies and lightning strikes up to 6 of them near your path, each once. You no longer need to touch them.
  • The hangar shows a short animated demo of each ship's dash.
  • Performance improvements: smoother big fights, especially with lots of pickups and chain lightning on screen.
  • Fixed: the Dreadnought Salvo evolution never fired. Packhunter missiles spread over more targets, and Absolute Zero Field shatters enemies it kills with its first pulse.
  • A new title screen when the game starts, and a new game icon.

2026-09-30

  • WATCH now shows the action: your turrets turn and fire like in the game, shots and chain lightning fly, enemies burst when they die, and boss and elite attacks are called out with their warning shapes. Replays from older versions still play, without the action.
  • Void Stalkers on the first planet hit 10% softer.

4.23 Orbit Overrun, and the Forge Titan reborn

2026-09-29

  • The game has a new name: ORBIT OVERRUN. Your progress, settings and replays carry over as they are.
  • The Forge Titan fights with four arms that move on their own and lose one each phase: Piston Slam, Saw Sweep, a Magnetic Grapple (dash to snap the chain), FLOOR MELTDOWN (step into the wedge that just erupted) and, near the end, OVERHEAT VENT (hug it or dash through the heat ring). At 70% the furnace opens and slag pools land for good; at 35% its core cracks. Its death is a white-hot burst.
  • New Foundry elite moves: the Hammer Elite bulldozes a lane of enemies at you and drops its hammer up close; the Slag Bombard lays a line of shells that burns, and punishes standing still.
  • Both bosses now have the same rhythm: two moves at first, then a new move and a big moment in each later phase. Moves that repeated others are gone.
  • The Commander has its own look: a void general with a cape and a glowing visor.
  • Fixes: augment cards no longer spill text off the card; boss hazards at a phase change always warn first; the Titan's slag stays when you continue a run; a few replay fixes.

4.22 The Colossus's final phase, new elite moves

2026-09-29

  • The Eclipse Colossus has a real third phase: TOTALITY. Its claws stab back in, it cracks and the world goes dark, and it pulls you toward it. New moves: Gravity Well (walk against the pull between the claw lines) and SHARD RAIN (stand in a pool of light; enemies caught in a pool die).
  • Its death is a moment now: it collapses to a point and a white burst wipes the screen.
  • The Commander has new moves: Rally Banner (its troops speed up until you kill it), Shield Wall (break the shield to stagger it) and a heavier lunge.
  • The Core Warden has Ground Pound (stand between the cracks) and, when hurt, runs away before turning to lunge.
  • The Rift Artillerist lays rifts that leave dart mines, sweeps an Anchor Beam if you get close, and blinks away when hurt.
  • Each new signature move shows a one-line tip the first time you see it.

4.21 Watch your replays

2026-09-29

  • New: WATCH your replays. MORE > REPLAYS has a WATCH button on each run: it plays the run back on its planet with your ship and the enemies around you, hits, level-ups and boss fights, with a timeline you can drag and speeds from 0.5x to 4x.
  • Replays now record everything on screen so they look like the real fight, while staying small (about 1 MB for a busy 10-minute run).

4.20 Crazy builds: tunings without caps

2026-09-29

  • Tunings have no level cap any more: once you own one you can keep taking it at full strength. Build as far into damage, crit, luck or hull as you like.
  • More tuning slots: 4 on the first planet (was 3) and 6 on the second (was 4).
  • Crit chance has no cap: past 100% your crits turn into super crits (150% crit means every hit crits and half of them hit for 3x).
  • Luck has no cap: it keeps making rare cards and the extra fourth card more likely, a little less with each point.
  • Fire Rate past the weapons' top speed now turns into damage instead of being wasted.
  • Move speed, armor and dash recharge still have limits, and projectile size stops at 4x, so the ship stays controllable.
  • Augment cards no longer show a rarity: it never changed what they did.

4.19 Replays; new Colossus and Ravager animations

Turn recording off with Settings > Record Replays.

4.18 The Eclipse Colossus and the Void Ravager, reworked; a cleaner main menu

2026-09-29

  • The Eclipse Colossus fights like a real boss now: it rakes with its claws, lunges through you leaving a dark strip, and throws shards where you are heading. At 70% its claws tear off and circle it as blades. In its second phase it snaps the claws across you and plunges the screen into darkness except a ring of light around itself: fight it in the light (it takes more damage there), then hug it or dash through the burst.
  • Every boss attack warns you first (at least 0.7 s, longer for the hard hits), no single hit takes more than 20% of your hull (25% for the big moment), and everything can be dashed through. Touching the boss hurts less (15, was 24), and extra enemies stop arriving around its big moment.
  • The Void Ravager is a bull: it paws the ground and charges (a miss leaves it stunned and open), crouches and flings you if you stand on it (back off when it crouches), and below half health it roars and charges three times.
  • Each new attack shows a one-line hint the first time you see it.
  • The main menu is reorganized: one column on the left (the eclipse fills the right), with PLAY and ENDLESS side by side, HANGAR, PILOT, SETTINGS, MORE and QUIT. PILOT is one page with tabs for your profile, the leaderboard, achievements and the codex (the leaderboard is in one place now). MORE holds the patch notes, credits, run reports and feedback.
  • Your pilot name is now protected on the leaderboard: it belongs to the device that claimed it.

4.15 Pilot profile, Hellfire bursts

2026-09-29

Each bolt is short, molten and piercing and leaves burning ground where they land. Elites take extra; Multi-Cannon adds a bolt per volley. Same damage as before.

4.14.2 Focus fire, a planet 2 that lasts, a real supply cache

2026-09-29

  • Tap or click an enemy to focus it: for 5 seconds every weapon that can reach it, and your drone, shoot it (amber bracket and timer). Right click works too on a computer.
  • SMART targeting now goes for a Null Leech that is draining you first, and prefers ranged shooters over chasers.
  • Your drone now fires at whatever your main weapon is aiming at (with Hunter Protocol it still takes elites first).
  • Supply caches are worth grabbing: they repair 35% of your hull (was 12%), give 60 scrap plus 6 for every minute into the planet (was 30), and shove nearby enemies back. Opening one is a proper loot moment now.
  • Hellfire Lance is now three separate lances, each locked and held on its own target (the single beam sweeping back and forth is gone). An elite or boss held by a lance takes triple.
  • Multi-Cannon Array now works on every evolution (it did nothing to them before): each tier adds a Hellfire lance, a Packhunter missile, a Reaper glaive, a Starbreaker follow-up rail, a Resonance Cascade jump, a Frozen Singularity hole or an Absolute Zero ice comet, or 3 Prism pellets.
  • Evolution balance: the Dimensional Reaper and Frozen Singularity were far ahead and hit less hard now; Packhunter, Starbreaker, Prism Crossfire and Absolute Zero hit harder. Prism pellets with Warhead Fabricator now pop once, small, instead of on every hit and ricochet (that combo did over three times any other evolution's damage).
  • You now see your ship fly into the dropship's bay, and the carrier lift off with you in it.
  • Planet 2 lasts longer: XP there is halved (a strong build used to be complete 2-3 minutes in, then 20 levels of leftovers), and the Forge Titan has more health, which keeps growing with your level past 30. A good build should now take about a minute or more to bring it down, not 18 seconds. The Eclipse Colossus has 25% more health too (with Multi-Cannon now powering the evolutions it fell in under 30 seconds).
  • Augment cards now say what they IMPROVE (your weapons they change) and what they EVOLVE, on separate lines (Mass Driver said 'evolves Railgun, Scattergun' where the weapons it improves should be). They only say EVOLUTION READY when taking the card makes an evolution possible.
  • Fixed wrong numbers in weapon texts: Hunter Missiles, Photon Scattergun, Graviton Mines and Arc Coil levels, the Plasma Lance's tiers, the Dimensional Reaper and Phase Step descriptions, and the ship blurbs.
  • Planet 1's timer names its own boss (Colossus, not Titan), and it is no longer called the final boss.
  • Run report: Catalyst and evolution times are run times (they were each planet's own clock), and crit chance like 0.5% no longer shows as 1%.

4.14 Progression across planets, dropship boons

2026-09-29

  • Evolutions are now spread across the run: one evolution is allowed on planet 1, and each new planet allows one more. A second Catalyst found on planet 1 is kept, and you can use it the moment you land on planet 2.
  • Every planet now has a Core Warden (as tough as that planet) dropping a Catalyst, and each planet's merchant can sell one. Before this, planet 2 had no way to evolve at all.
  • When you launch the dropship you now pick one of three boons for the rest of the run: Spare Catalyst Bay (+1 evolution), Catalyst On Board, Augment Bay (+1 augment slot), Tuning Bay, Warden Beacon (the next Warden comes early), Supply Drop (scrap), Merchant Contract (earlier, cheaper merchants next planet), Elite Bounty or Reinforced Hull.
  • Merchant prices start fresh on each planet (planet 2's were still climbing from planet 1's visits: a Catalyst would have cost 1,344 scrap). Prices now rise 25% a planet instead.
  • The HUD tells you when a Catalyst is saved for the next planet, and the run report lists your boons and how many evolutions you used.
  • After a hit your ship is now safe for half a second (was a third): getting surrounded no longer melts the hull as fast.
  • Fewer ranged enemies early on planet 1 (about 5 at 4:00 instead of 8), back to normal by 6:00.
  • Null Leeches now show what they do: a violet tether crackles to your ship while one drains you, and the hull you lose shows as a red number. Kill them or step away (their drain reaches about 140 px, and it stops your hull repair while it lasts).

4.13.2 New dash bursts, Nova's early game, softer curses

2026-09-28

  • Dashing with Void Blades (Voidrunner, or any ship with the Phase Tether relic) no longer leaves your blades behind: they stay with you and burst outward where you land, hitting and shoving the pack.
  • The Dimensional Reaper no longer flings two glaives along your dash: all three stay with you and burst outward where you land, harder than the blades.
  • Reaper throws now go for the nearest enemy closing in, hit much harder and knock enemies back. The red tear lines they left behind are gone.
  • Every Multi-Cannon Array tier now adds a Void Blade (the third one added nothing).
  • Plasma Lance hits harder at low tiers and starts burning from tier 2, so Nova's early game holds up (in our test runs Nova now reaches the boss instead of dying at 4-6 minutes).
  • The Black Market's two harshest deals are softer: Glass Reactor is now +60% damage for -30% hull (was +100% / -50%), Void Core +40% XP for enemies 10% faster (was +50% / 20%).

4.13.1 Arc Coil, Scattergun and their evolutions hit harder

2026-09-28

  • Arc Coil (Tempest's weapon) was by far the weakest weapon: alone in a planet 1 horde at 7:00 it did about a third of Plasma Lance's damage and a fifth of its kills. Its zaps now hit harder, fire more often and lose much less strength as they jump through a pack, so it clears packed groups. At its top tier it now matches Plasma Lance.
  • Resonance Cascade (evolved Arc Coil) does over twice the damage it did.
  • Photon Scattergun pellets hit harder from tier 2 up (up to +50% at tier 5).
  • Prism Crossfire (evolved Scattergun) was weaker than the gun it evolves from: its pellets now pass through an enemy so the light web actually forms in a crowd, and both the pellets and the web hit much harder (about 4.5 times the damage it did).

4.13 New Void Blades, three Reaper glaives

2026-09-28

  • Void Blades have new art: hooked crimson shurikens with a glowing red core that spin as they orbit you (they were red crescents with a gear). Easier to see on dark ground.
  • The Dimensional Reaper now has three glaives circling you instead of two, drawn as great four-bladed shurikens, the grown-up form of the new Void Blades.
  • A dash still hurls two glaives down either side of your path; the third now stays home, circling and guarding the ship.
  • Each circling glaive cuts a little less, so the ring as a whole hits about as hard as before.

4.12 Black Market, drone chips, the carrier

2026-09-28

  • A Black Market dealer now visits once per planet (between 3:15 and 4:15) and stays for 60 seconds. It's a rusty freighter with red lamps, marked at the edge of your screen. Fly onto it to trade.
  • It sells drone chips, new upgrades for your companion drone. You get 2 chip slots, and a 3rd once your drone has Hunter Protocol. You can buy one chip per visit, and if your slots are full you pick which chip it replaces.
  • The six chips: Guardian Screen (your drone blocks a bullet every 6 s), Salvage Sweep (it pulls nearby XP and scrap to you), Field Medic (collecting scrap repairs a little hull), Chain Bolts (drone shots arc to a second enemy), Dash Link (dashing powers up the drone's next shot) and Decoy Beacon (dashing leaves a false drone that explodes).
  • The dealer also offers risky deals, one per visit and each once per run: Glass Reactor, Void Core, Hull Mortgage (scrap now for less hull) and Heavy Capacitors (more damage, slower dash).
  • The dropship after a boss is now a big carrier, about three ship lengths long. It comes down from the sky with its engines blazing and lands with a shockwave. Fly into its lit docking bay to leave.
  • The merchant's relic stall is now called RELIC (the name Black Market belongs to the new dealer).

4.11 Planet 2 picks up where planet 1 left off, gem tiers

2026-09-28

  • Planet 2 no longer starts over from an easy 0:00: its enemies pick up close to where planet 1 left off (a Foundry enemy at 0:00 now has about 170 health instead of 34, and is worth about twice the XP), and the crowd grows faster than on planet 1, so the Foundry is busy from the start and you keep levelling up (about twice as fast as before in our tests).
  • XP gems come in tiers: green, blue from 25 XP, gold from 100, white from 400. Gems lying close together merge into bigger ones, so fewer gems cover the ground (less lag in big fights) and no XP is lost.
  • Planet 2 has bigger break-out rings (at 0:40, 3:30 and 6:30), with armored enemies in them and one gap to escape through.
  • Planet 2 opens with its full share of ranged enemies.
  • The run report lists every pick you made with its time and level, a line per planet (time, levels, kills, damage taken, lowest FPS) and the map's seed.

4.10 Void Blades rebalance, boss banners, ship traits

2026-09-28

  • Void Blades no longer make you untouchable: they cut up to 2 enemy bullets, then one more every 0.75 s, and only the ring itself cuts, so an enemy that slips inside can reach you. Extra-projectile upgrades add at most 2 blades, Area grows the orbit more gently (up to a limit), and the top-tier shockwave is smaller.
  • Void Blades no longer freeze elites with every cut, so the Core Warden and other elites can fight back.
  • Beating a boss now shows a big BOSS DEFEATED banner, and arriving on a new planet shows its name in the middle of the screen (it used to be a small message that got replaced straight away).
  • Planet 2 now has its scheduled elites at 2:00, 5:00 and 8:00 (they never came), and the line under the timer shows the wave again instead of FINAL BOSS.
  • Ship traits now work: they grow with your level up to a cap (Nova +20% damage, Voidrunner +10% speed and +15% blade reach, Aegis +20% area, Tempest +15% fire rate). Before this they never applied in a real run.
  • New relic TARGETING RELAY replaces Orbit Drones: every 6 s your drone marks the strongest enemy near you, and everything you have deals +15% damage to it for 3 s.
  • When the boss dies, the enemy shots in the air vanish with it.
  • The speed test page (bench.html) has a COPY RESULTS button, so you can paste the results as text. It works on a computer's browser too.

4.09 Every enemy animated

2026-09-28

  • Every enemy, elite and boss on both planets now has real frame-by-frame animation, cut from its own painted picture: legs step, blades sway, treads roll, cores pulse.
  • Their attacks show in their bodies: wind-ups glow and hold while a charge is held, charges stretch and streak, shots kick back, the Phase Stalker and Stalker Unit break into scanlines as they blink.
  • Enemies that hurt you by touch now visibly bite, slash or ram when they reach your ship.
  • Small enemies keep their animations at a lighter size in memory, so phones and tablets load and run them without extra strain.

4.08 Name box fix, slow-device fairness

4.07 Leaderboard on the menu, faster Foundry on phones

2026-09-28

  • The phone speed test (bench.html) works with the phone held upright too.

4.06 Boss fight and dropship fixes

2026-09-27

  • The dropship takes you now: flying over its pad is enough (the pad is bigger and boards in a third of a second). Before, you had to sit still on a small pad for almost a second, which a crowd rarely allowed.
  • When a planet's boss arrives, the swarm bursts away and you face the boss on a clear field. A Core Warden still carrying its catalyst stays.
  • During the boss fight, at most 24 other enemies are around at once, and they stay as strong as when the boss arrived instead of getting tougher every minute.
  • Bosses have less health on Hard and Brutal (x1.25 and x1.5 instead of x1.5 and x2; regular enemies are unchanged).
  • A long boss fight wears the boss down: after 2:30 it takes more damage every minute (double at 4:30, triple at 6:30), so no fight drags on for ten minutes.
  • Run reports: the boss fight time is right after SAVE & QUIT and CONTINUE (it showed the whole run).

4.05 Global leaderboard
